# Company Handbook (Sample Knowledge Base)

## Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday. Employees
may request flexible timing with manager approval, provided they complete 8
hours of work per day.

## Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 18 days of paid leave per calendar year, plus 10
public holidays. Sick leave beyond 3 consecutive days requires a medical
certificate. Unused leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days
into the next year.

## Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. Fully remote arrangements
require director-level approval and are reviewed every 6 months. All remote
employees must be reachable during core hours (11 AM to 4 PM).

## Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses under $50 can be self-approved and submitted through the
expense portal within 30 days. Expenses above $50 require prior manager
approval. Travel expenses must include itemized receipts.

## Onboarding Process
New hires complete a 5-day onboarding program covering company policies,
tools setup, and role-specific training. IT equipment is provisioned before
the employee's start date. A 90-day probation review is conducted with the
reporting manager.

## Performance Reviews
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in June and December. Reviews
include self-assessment, manager assessment, and peer feedback. Ratings feed
into the annual compensation review cycle.
